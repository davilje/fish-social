# Pastel fishing scene layers and motion

The 30 pastel panoramas (six fishing regions, five variants each) each have nine scene layers: sky, three cloud depths, distant scenery, middle scenery, moving foliage, shore, and water. All 30 also include `leaf-wind-weight.png` and `motion.json`. Unity art lives in `fish-social-unity/Assets/Desktop/OverlayArt/Ponds/layered/pastel-scenes-v1/`.

Twenty existing pond layouts use 16 of these scenes. The other 14 are imported Unity art candidates; no new pond assignments were made. `pond-calm` retains its separately animated Qiandao scene. `OverlayPondArtBaker.ApplyRemainingLayeredScenesAndExport()` refreshes the 20 assigned prefabs and desktop Overlay exports without moving gameplay objects. `ImportUnassignedPastelSceneSprites()` imports the other 14 candidates without adding menu entries.

`motion.json` version 2 names three cloud layers. Overlay repeats each cloud bitmap twice in one clipped viewport and moves both copies to the right at 1.6, 3.2, and 5.2 source pixels per second. The second copy fills the edge as the first leaves; modulo travel makes the sequence continuous. The foliage layer uses a grayscale wind map to displace painted leaves while keeping trunks and roots near zero displacement. The maximum foliage displacement is 7.2 source pixels, evaluated at 24 FPS when the scene is visible. Water remains a separate art layer; the existing Overlay water effects are independent.

The visible colors and textures come from the approved 2172 x 724 source panoramas. The animation cutouts require limited sky and middle-ground infill behind moving pixels, so the new rest frame is visually close to the source rather than bit-identical. The exported layer layouts keep the existing 1680 x 560 coordinate system and `groundY` for each map.

Generation and validation artifacts are in `/Users/red/Desktop/fish/output/scene-revision/pastel-motion-30-v1/`. Validation covered all 30 nine-layer manifests; all three cloud layers and wind masks contain painted pixels in every scene. The 20 exported layouts preserve all non-scenery objects and waterlines from the pre-install snapshot. Unity imported 30 sets and exported the assigned 20; the Overlay scene-motion test and WPF project build passed. A real-size foliage sampling benchmark on `river-01-mint` took about 7.8 ms per frame on this machine, excluding WPF upload and composition.
